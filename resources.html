Session Setup & Tools

Everything you need for upcoming sessions.

Follow these step-by-step setup guides to configure your free accounts and software tools before joining our live Data Analytics classes.

Google Sheets BigQuery (GCP) Looker Studio Power BI

Google Sheets

Used for rapid data cleaning, pivot tables, lookup formulas, and business calculations in our foundational sessions.

  • 1 Sign in with any personal Google / Gmail account.
  • 2 No software installation required — works 100% free in your browser.
  • 3 Ensure access to Google Drive to download session practice CSV files.
Open Google Sheets →

Google BigQuery

Google Cloud's enterprise data warehouse. We use the 100% Free Sandbox tier (no credit card required) for SQL querying.

  • 1 Go to Google Cloud Console and navigate to BigQuery.
  • 2 Create a new GCP Project (e.g., kologs-data-analytics).
  • 3 Enable BigQuery Sandbox (gives 10 GB free storage & 1 TB free query analysis per month).
Setup BigQuery Free Sandbox →

Looker Studio

Google's free web-based BI tool for building interactive live dashboards connected directly to Sheets or BigQuery.

  • 1 Open Looker Studio with your Google account.
  • 2 Complete the one-time welcome setup & accept terms.
  • 3 Practice creating a blank report and linking a sample Google Sheet dataset.
Launch Looker Studio →

Power BI Desktop

Microsoft's flagship business intelligence application for end-to-end data modeling, DAX, and report creation.

  • 1 Power BI Desktop is 100% FREE for Windows users.
  • 2 Download directly from Microsoft Store or official site.
  • 3 Mac Users: Can follow along during live sessions or run Windows via Parallels / virtual desktop.
Download Power BI Desktop →
